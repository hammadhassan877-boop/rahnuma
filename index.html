About

One coach, two tracks, built for Pakistan specifically.

Rahnuma runs as two guided conversations: a Career Coach that matches your strengths and constraints to real Pakistani employers and PKR salary ranges, and a Scholarship Coach that checks provincial and international funding against your CGPA, income, and IELTS status.

Knows their options

Real employers, real scholarship bodies. Systems Ltd, NetSol, HBL, and Daraz sit next to PEEF, SEEF, and KPEF, not advice imported from somewhere else.

Believes they qualify

Never self-impose a limitation before you have checked. Rahnuma always finds what you do qualify for, from district quotas to internal university funds.

Chooses their own direction

Three paths, not one verdict. A guide points, it doesn't push you toward a destination it already decided on.
